# Delete Your Account & Data — Pro Construction Calculator

If you'd like to delete your account and the data associated with it (your Google sign-in link, synced project data, and blueprint files stored in the cloud), you have two options.

## Delete individual cloud projects (in-app)
Open the app, go to your project list, and tap Delete on any project with Cloud Sync enabled.
- If you're the project owner, this permanently removes that project's data, blueprint files, and invite code from our cloud storage.
- If you're a team member, this removes your access and email from that project.

## Request full account deletion
To request deletion of all your account data across every synced project, email us at **djransom1@gmail.com** from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete My Account – Pro Construction Calculator". We will delete:
- Your account's association with any cloud-synced projects
- Any project data and blueprint files you own in our cloud storage (Firebase Firestore and Storage)

We will complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email once complete.
