Privacy Policy for Pro Construction Calculator
Last Updated: July 18, 2026
1. Introduction
Pro Construction Calculator ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through the App.
2. Information Collection and Use
Personal Information: We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, or phone number. App Data: Any data you enter into the app, including project names, construction measurements, and S-Pen sketches, is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data, and it is not uploaded to our servers.
3. In-App Purchases and Billing
The App uses Google Play Billing for subscriptions and purchases. All financial transactions are handled by Google. We do not see or store your credit card information or billing address. Google provides us with an anonymous "purchase token" to verify that your Pro Access is active.
4. Permissions
The App may request the following permissions:
•
Storage/Media: Required only to save and load your S-Pen sketches and PDF reports locally on your phone.
•
Internet: Used exclusively by the Google Play Billing Library to verify your subscription status.
5. Data Deletion
Because your data is stored locally, you have full control over it. You can delete individual projects within the app, or delete all data by uninstalling the App from your device.
6. Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. Your usage patterns are not tracked or shared.
