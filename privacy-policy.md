# Privacy Policy for Construction Calculator & Formulas

**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026

## Overview
Your privacy is important to us. **Construction Calculator & Formulas** is designed to respect and protect your privacy while providing powerful construction, carpentry, and field calculation tools. By default, the app works entirely offline and stores everything only on your device. Signing in and enabling Cloud Sync for a project are both optional, Pro-tier features — if you never sign in, no account or project data ever leaves your device.

## Data Collection and Usage

### If you don't sign in (default)
- **No Personal Data Collected:** The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **Local Storage Only:** All project data, custom formulas, notes, calculation histories, blueprint PDFs, and exported takeoffs are stored exclusively on your local device.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics, telemetry, tracking tools, or advertising networks that monitor your activity inside the app.

### If you sign in and use Cloud Sync (optional, Pro feature)
When you choose to sign in with Google and enable Cloud Sync on a project, the app collects and stores the following in order to provide that feature:
- **Account information:** Your Google account email address and a unique account identifier, obtained through Google Sign-In.
- **Project data:** The calculator entries, notes, and other data for any project you specifically enable Cloud Sync on.
- **Blueprint files:** Any PDF blueprint files attached to a Cloud Sync–enabled project.

This data is stored using Google's Firebase platform (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) so that your synced projects can appear across your own devices. Projects you don't enable Cloud Sync on are never uploaded.

### Team members
If you invite others to a project (or join one via an invite code), be aware that:
- Everyone with access to that project can see its calculator data, notes, and blueprint files.
- Your email address becomes visible to other members of that specific shared project, and theirs to you.
- The project's owner can remove members, and removing yourself ("leaving" a project) does not delete it for other members.

## Permissions Used
The app may request standard system permissions necessary for its operational features:
- **Storage / Media Access:** Used to save, export, or open project PDFs and calculation notes on your device.
- **Internet Access:** Used only when you sign in and use Cloud Sync, to communicate with Google's Firebase services.

## Third-Party Services
- **Local-only use:** The app does not share any data with third-party services, vendors, or external servers.
- **Cloud Sync (optional):** If you enable Cloud Sync, your account and project data are processed by **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage) in order to provide that feature. Google's handling of this data is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data Deletion
- Deleting a project you own that has Cloud Sync enabled also deletes its data, blueprint files, and invite code from our cloud storage.
- Leaving a shared project removes your access and email from that project, but doesn't delete it for its owner or other members.
- You can stop using Cloud Sync entirely at any time by signing out; this does not delete projects already synced to the cloud — delete them individually first if you want their cloud copies removed.
- To request deletion of your account data in a way not covered by the options above, contact us using the information below, or see our [account deletion page](https://github.com/djransom1-cpu/pro-construction-calculator/blob/main/delete-account.md).

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
